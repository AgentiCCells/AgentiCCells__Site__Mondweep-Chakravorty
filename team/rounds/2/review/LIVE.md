## Does round 2 meet Dinis's aim?

**Largely, yes.** Against the Marc Noah home, which leads with a full-bleed photograph and playable films, this site has only a 200 px headshot to work with, so it has rightly made the proof of concept its picture. The home now has a strong first screen (teal field, the headshot with its offset block and caption, a very large name, the run drawn as a motif, four number tiles) and the run on a true time scale as its centrepiece. Each design now has a first screen of its own: Broadsheet's Plate 1 and caption column, Run log's story and terminal, Blueprint's zoned sheet with balloons and a title block, and Vault's dial, which is the most original drawing on the site. That is the level of the other AgentiCCells sites.
