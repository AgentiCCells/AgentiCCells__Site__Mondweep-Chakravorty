# Mondweep Chakravorty

Mondweep Chakravorty: enterprise AI leader and agentic systems builder in London, co-founder of Agentics Foundation London, and his work with Dinis Cruz.

Pages are generated at build time from `data/site.mjs` (words) and `research/facts.json` (facts, each with a quote and read date) by `scripts/generate.mjs`, `designs.mjs` (five designs under /d/) and `behind.mjs` (/behind-the-scenes/). Every fact links to its public source. The team that runs the site, its policies and round reports are in `team/`; start with `team/WORKFLOW.md` and `team/HANDOVER.md`.

- Live at https://mondweep-chakravorty.agenticcells.site (version at /version.json).
- Made by the AgentiCCells site factory from request `requests/2026-11-18__mondweep-chakravorty/` in AgentiCCells__Site__Factory, seeded from AgentiCCells__Site__Template.
- Release: `scripts/release.sh` (build, validate, leak scan, then push to dev; CI deploys and tags v0.1.N).
